Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Effective Date: September 2026

🛡️ Summary Commitment

WakeWalk does not collect, transmit, sell, or store any personal data on external servers.

WakeWalk is open-source software engineered under the MIT License. The application functions entirely offline. Your alarms, schedules, step counter readings, and scanned barcode values are processed exclusively on your local device and never leave your hardware.

📱 Android Permissions Used & Rationale

WakeWalk requests only the minimum permissions strictly necessary to execute reliable, anti-cheat alarm functionality:

Permission Purpose
USE_EXACT_ALARM / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Required by Android 12–15 to wake the device at the exact millisecond requested, bypassing Doze mode.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_HEALTH Allows the alarm service to keep playing audio and counting steps continuously over the lockscreen without being killed by the OS.
USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT Displays the ringing screen immediately when the device screen is off or locked.
ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION Accesses hardware step sensors (Step Counter / Step Detector) to verify physical walking.
CAMERA Used exclusively in the Barcode/QR challenge to scan household items. Camera frames are analyzed in real-time on-device via ML Kit and are never recorded or stored.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Reschedules your active alarms automatically if your device restarts overnight or updates.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Required on Android 13+ to display upcoming alarm notices and persistent ringing controls.

💾 Local Data Storage

All app state is saved strictly in local device databases:

  • Room Database: Alarm configurations, recurring day masks, active alarm session state, and morning streak counts.
  • DataStore Preferences: User settings such as default step counts, ringtone preferences, and OLED theme choices.

Uninstalling the application or clearing app data permanently removes all stored data from your device.

🌐 Website Analytics (Landing Page Only)

The marketing website (rktiwari00.github.io/Alarmee) uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) solely to track anonymous visitor counts, general referral sources (e.g. GitHub, Reddit), and download button clicks to help the developer measure adoption. IP addresses are anonymized by default. The Android mobile app itself contains zero analytics SDKs.
