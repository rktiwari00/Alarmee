Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about wake-up reliability, anti-cheat detection, and device permissions.

Yes, absolutely. WakeWalk routes audio using Android's dedicated STREAM_ALARM channel with AudioAttributes.USAGE_ALARM. By Android operating system design, alarm streams intentionally bypass ringer mute, silent switch, and vibration-only modes to play out loud through your device speaker at full volume.

WakeWalk registers system broadcast receivers for RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED and TIME_SET. As soon as your device finishes booting or your timezone changes, WakeWalk automatically recalculates the exact trigger times and reschedules all active alarms with the OS AlarmManager.

No. WakeWalk features a multi-tier anti-cheat movement validator. It enforces a maximum human cadence threshold (rejecting steps occurring faster than 320ms or >3.1 Hz) and analyzes 3D acceleration variance. Rapid shaking, pillow hitting, or bed vibrating are rejected as counterfeit steps.

Custom Android skins (Samsung One UI, Xiaomi HyperOS/MIUI, OnePlus OxygenOS) include aggressive proprietary battery killers that sleep foreground apps overnight. To guarantee 100% alarm delivery, open the in-app Settings → Diagnostics dashboard and set WakeWalk's Battery Optimization to Unrestricted.

WakeWalk includes a safety dismissal mechanism for emergencies. You can type the deliberate phrase "I AM AWAKE" or hold the emergency button continuously for 5 seconds. Additionally, system Emergency SOS shortcuts, power keys, and incoming phone calls remain completely accessible.

Zero. The mobile app is 100% on-device and privacy-first. There are no user accounts, no login requirements, and no analytics SDKs in the Android app. All alarms, streak records, and barcode references reside exclusively in your local SQLite/Room database.
